Middle East

Dome of the Rock & Al-Aqsa Compound

The golden Dome of the Rock on Jerusalem’s Haram al-Sharif / Temple Mount — non-Muslim visiting hours are limited and changeable; security and modest dress are mandatory.

Islamic · Jerusalem

Middle East

Hagia Sophia

Justinian’s great church, now Ayasofya Grand Mosque — ticketed upper-gallery visitor route for tourists, free worship access, facing the Blue Mosque in Sultanahmet.

Islamic · Christian · Türkiye

Middle East

Church of the Holy Sepulchre

Jerusalem’s Church of the Holy Sepulchre — free entry to the traditional sites of Calvary and the Tomb, shared by multiple Christian communities under Status Quo rules.

Christian · Jerusalem

Middle East

Western Wall (Kotel)

Judaism’s most visited prayer site — the retaining wall of the Temple Mount, open day and night after security screening in Jerusalem’s Old City.

Jewish · Israel

Middle East

Sultan Ahmed Mosque (Blue Mosque)

Istanbul’s six-minaret imperial mosque facing Hagia Sophia — free visitor entry outside prayer times, with modest dress and shoe removal required.

Islamic · Türkiye

Middle East

Church of the Nativity

UNESCO basilica on Manger Square — traditional birthplace of Jesus and a primary Holy Land pilgrimage stop under shared Christian custodianship; Grotto queues are the real time cost.

Christian · Palestine

Middle East

Petra (Al-Khazneh sacred landscape)

Rose-red Nabataean capital and UNESCO World Heritage landscape in Jordan — Al-Khazneh beyond the Siq, ticketed via PDTRA PetraPass / visitpetra.jo channels.

Nabataean · Jordan

Middle East

Baháʼí Gardens (Terraces of the Shrine of the Báb), Haifa

UNESCO Baháʼí Holy Places on Mount Carmel — free sacred-garden terraces; shrine vs viewpoint hours differ. Official: ganbahai.org.il (third-party "tickets" are tours, not admission).

Baháʼí · Israel

Middle East

Saint Catherine's Monastery, Mount Sinai

UNESCO Orthodox monastery at the foot of Mount Sinai — ultra-short visitor window; typically closed Fri/Sun/feasts. Official: sinaimonastery.com. Grounds generally free; summit hike is separate.

Christian · Egypt

Middle East

Göbekli Tepe, Şanlıurfa

UNESCO Neolithic ceremonial precinct (~9600 BCE T-pillars) — world’s highest-demand “oldest temple” archaeology draw. Official: muze.gov.tr (Göbeklitepe Örenyeri); combined site + animation ticket often sold at the gişe. Do not invent TRY/EUR fares.

Prehistoric · Türkiye

Middle East

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Abu Dhabi

UAE’s signature mosque and global tourist magnet. Visitors book an online access pass via Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Centre (szgmc.gov.ae); worshippers enter designated gates without a tourist pass. Friday visitor windows pause for midday prayer. Modest dress / abaya when required. Do not invent AED; prefer official SZGMC over resellers.

Islamic · United Arab Emirates

Middle East

Imam Mosque / Shah Mosque (مسجد امام), Naqsh-e Jahan, Isfahan

UNESCO Meidan Emam Safavid congregational masterpiece on Naqsh-e Jahan’s south side. Square free; mosque separate heritage ticket (Iranian vs foreign tiers). Friday / mourning / iʿtikāf may close tourist entry. Sheikh Lotfollah + Ali Qapu are separate products. Foreign cards often fail — cash IRR honesty. Do not invent IRR/USD.

Islamic · Iran
