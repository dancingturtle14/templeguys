Sanctuary of Saint Pio of Pietrelcina / Santa Maria delle Grazie, San Giovanni Rotondo

San Giovanni Rotondo, Gargano / Foggia province, Apulia

Quick facts

Founded
20th-century Capuchin pilgrimage centre around Padre Pio of Pietrelcina; modern Chiesa San Pio (Renzo Piano) alongside older Santa Maria delle Grazie
Visit length
Half-day to full day sanctuary circuit (old + new church, crypt, optional museum); pilgrimage coaches common from Foggia / Bari / Naples corridors
UNESCO
Not a standalone World Heritage listing (major modern pilgrimage sanctuary)
Patron / era
Catholic — Saint Pio of Pietrelcina (Padre Pio); stigmata devotion and pilgrimage sanctuary

Entry

Free entry

Churches free; museum/group products separate; do not invent EUR; ≠ Assisi/Loreto/Vatican combo.

Convento Santuario Padre Pio (conventosantuariopadrepio.it) — churches generally free; verify Orari / pellegrinaggi →

Hours

Sanctuary / crypt / museum windows — verify official Orari pages on conventosantuariopadrepio.it. Early morning Mass quieter; feast days of Padre Pio reshape queues and town lodging. Published patterns commonly list Santa Maria delle Grazie long open hours and Chiesa San Pio daytime windows.

Tickets

Churches generally free. Museum / house / guided-group products as published via Ufficio Pellegrinaggi — do not invent EUR. Prefer conventosantuariopadrepio.it over scalper packages. Assisi / Loreto / Vatican = separate pilgrimages.

Dress

Active major Catholic pilgrimage shrine — modest dress in churches; quiet at tomb / crypt; no flash where posted; follow volunteer steward lanes.

Duration

Half-day to full day for old + new church, crypt, and optional museum lanes.

Access

Foggia / Bari / Naples road and coach gateways to San Giovanni Rotondo on the Gargano. Sanctuary complex is walkable in town once arrived. Not the same ticket as Assisi or Padua.

Tips

  • Confirm Orari on conventosantuariopadrepio.it — do not invent EUR.
  • Map old Santa Maria delle Grazie vs new Chiesa San Pio before you arrive.
  • Early morning quieter; feast days = lodging and queue peaks.
  • Modest dress; quiet at crypt / tomb; follow steward lanes.
  • Do not invent one pass with Assisi, Loreto, or Vatican.
  • Ufficio Pellegrinaggi helps groups — individuals usually free-walk churches.
  • SanctaWay does not sell tickets or grant access.
