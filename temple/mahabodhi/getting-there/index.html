Getting there

How visitors usually reach Mahabodhi Temple in Bodh Gaya.

Fly into Patna (Jay Prakash Narayan Airport) or, less commonly, Gaya Airport when schedules allow, then continue by prepaid taxi or hired car to Bodh Gaya. Road time from Patna is often three to four hours depending on traffic and season — do not cut connections tight.

By train, Gaya Junction is the practical railhead on major lines. From Gaya, take a taxi, auto-rickshaw chain, or shared transport the remaining ~12–16 km to Bodh Gaya town. Within Bodh Gaya, the temple sits at the centre of the pilgrimage zone and is reachable on foot or by short auto/cycle-rickshaw from most guesthouses.

Once at the gate, allow time for security screening and shoe storage. If you arrive during a large Buddhist gathering, queues and one-way crowd flow can add 30–60 minutes.

  • — Rail: Gaya Junction → road to Bodh Gaya
  • — Air: Patna (common) or Gaya (seasonal/limited) → road transfer
  • — Local: auto-rickshaw / walk inside Bodh Gaya
  • — Carry small notes for rickshaws; agree fares before boarding shared autos
