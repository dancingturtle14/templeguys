Best time

Weekday mornings and official late Thursday hours help.

Weekday opening slots are usually kinder than weekend midday. Thursday evenings (when late hours apply) can offer a different pace after day-trip crowds leave.

Major feast days and school holidays lengthen every queue — reservation becomes essential, not optional.

  • Best: weekday morning reserved slot
  • Try Thursday late opening when listed
  • Avoid assuming walk-up is fast in peak season
  • Recheck hours around Christmas and Easter
