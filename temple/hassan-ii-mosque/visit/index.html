Hours

Seasonal guided morning/afternoon windows published by FMH2. Friday schedules are shorter; Ramadan hours change. Confirm same-week slots on fmh2.ma before you travel.

Tickets

Fondation de la Mosquée Hassan II (FMH2) — fmh2.ma Paid Guided Tour / Visites payantes. On-site / foundation channels; VIP private visits by reservation. Verify live MAD rates — do not invent fares.

Dress

Cover shoulders, torso, and below the knees. No shorts or sleeveless tops. Remove shoes (bags usually provided). Bring a scarf or wrap if you are unsure your outfit meets staff standards.

Duration

Plan about an hour for the guided circuit class of visit, plus security screening and ticket-line buffer on both sides.

Access

Active mosque — tourist circuit is scheduled around worship. Follow the guide; do not wander off the tour path. Windy Corniche approaches; expect security checks.

Tips

  • Confirm same-week hours and slots on fmh2.ma — Friday and Ramadan differ.
  • Arrive early for security and ticket lines.
  • Dress to cover shoulders, torso, and below knees before you reach the door.
  • Remove shoes when instructed; keep the provided bag with you.
  • No video cameras / noisy devices inside where FMH2 rules forbid them.
  • Stay with your guide group — this is not a free-roam visit.
  • Hammam and other facilities are separate products when offered.
