Bagan Archaeological Zone, Myanmar

UNESCO temple plain (~thousands of stupas/temples) — SE Asia’s other Angkor-scale sacred landscape. Zone fee collected at airport / checkpoints (Department of Archaeology); keep receipt. No climbing temples — use designated viewpoints. tourism.gov.mm context. Do not invent MMK; re-check travel advisories.

Pura Agung Besakih

Bali’s Mother Temple on Gunung Agung — living Agama Hindu Dharma complex. Official guidance besakih.org; foreign tickets often bundle sarong/sash + local guide + shuttle. Ceremony closures possible. Do not invent IDR; hard-warn roadside touts.

Southeast Asia

Batu Caves (Sri Subramaniar Swamy), Selangor

272-step Murugan staircase and giant statue — KL day-trip Hindu mega. Main Temple Cave / plaza climb generally free (Sri Subramaniar Swamy Devasthanam). Optional paid caves (Ramayana Cave, Cave Villa, Dark Cave when open) sold on-site. Shoulders + knees covered enforced at stairs. Do not invent MYR; do not treat Viator as official temple entry.

Southeast Asia

Bayon Temple, Angkor Thom, Siem Reap

Jayavarman VII face-tower state temple at the centre of Angkor Thom — global icon inside the same Angkor Pass as live Angkor Wat. Official Angkor Enterprise only: angkorenterprise.gov.kh / ticket.angkorenterprise.gov.kh. No separate Bayon gate ticket. Central tower may close for conservation. Do not invent USD; hard-warn street scalpers.

Southeast Asia

Wat Phra That Doi Suthep, Chiang Mai

Northern Thailand’s mountain Lanna pilgrimage chedi — Chiang Mai day-trip sacred mega. Temple foreign-visitor contribution collected on-site (Thai nationals often free when posted); funicular separate when running. Doi Suthep–Pui National Park fee (if charged on road for trails/park areas) is a different product from temple entry. Info via TAT tourismthailand.org. Do not invent THB. Stairs (~300 Naga steps) vs funicular honesty.

Mỹ Sơn Sanctuary, Quảng Nam (near Hội An / Đà Nẵng)

UNESCO Cham Hindu temple towers in a mountain valley — Vietnam’s sacred archaeological crown. Official BQL info disanvanhoamyson.vn; e-tickets mysonticketonline.vn. Foreign / domestic / child tiers — ticket commonly bundles museum + shuttle + Cham show + ruins when published. Do not invent VND; prefer BQL / official e-ticket over random OTA as primary CTA.

Ta Prohm (Rajavihara), Angkor, Siem Reap

World-famous strangler-fig / silk-cotton tree temple (Rajavihara) inside Angkor Archaeological Park — same Angkor Pass as live Angkor Wat and Bayon. Official Angkor Enterprise only: angkorenterprise.gov.kh / ticket.angkorenterprise.gov.kh. No separate Ta Prohm gate ticket. Conservation / one-way galleries honesty; do not invent USD; hard-warn street scalpers.

Southeast Asia

Kek Lok Si (極樂寺), Air Itam, Penang

Largest Buddhist temple complex in Malaysia — Pagoda of Ten Thousand Buddhas and Guanyin pavilion incline at Air Itam, Penang. Grounds commonly free; pagoda and inclined lift / Guanyin pavilion are separate on-site fares (cash common). Verify myPenang / on-site desks. No reliable single national e-ticket — do not invent MYR; do not treat random OTAs as required entry.

Southeast Asia

Ayutthaya Historical Park (พระนครศรีอยุธยา), Phra Nakhon Si Ayutthaya

UNESCO Historic City of Ayutthaya — Siamese Buddhist royal-temple ruins (Wat Phra Si Sanphet / Wat Mahathat / Wat Chaiwatthanaram…). Fine Arts Department Historical Park tickets: per-site or multi-site pass products (Thai vs foreign tiers) — verify live Fine Arts / TAT tourismthailand.org / on-site desks. Do not invent THB; do not treat random OTA “Ayutthaya pass” as Fine Arts without verify.
