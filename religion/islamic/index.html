Taj Mahal, Agra

UNESCO Mughal Islamic mausoleum — India's defining day-visit. Closed Fridays for general viewing. Official: tajmahal.gov.in + ASI e-tickets; hard-warn fake ticket sites. Do not invent INR.

Great Mosque of Kairouan (Mosque of Uqba), Tunisia

UNESCO Maghreb pilgrimage mosque — living courtyard tourism with clear non-Muslim rules (courtyard typical; prayer hall generally closed to non-Muslims). Official AMVPPC: patrimoinedetunisie.com.tn. On-site ticket common. Do not invent TND; Friday/Ramadan hours shrink.

Islamic · Tunisia

Europe

Alhambra and Generalife

UNESCO Nasrid palace-city above Granada — Alcazaba, timed Nasrid Palaces, Partal, Generalife. Official Patronato tickets only (tickets.alhambra-patronato.es). Nominative ID required. Do not invent EUR; avoid third-party Nasrid scalpers.

Middle East

Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Abu Dhabi

UAE’s signature mosque and global tourist magnet. Visitors book an online access pass via Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque Centre (szgmc.gov.ae); worshippers enter designated gates without a tourist pass. Friday visitor windows pause for midday prayer. Modest dress / abaya when required. Do not invent AED; prefer official SZGMC over resellers.

Islamic · United Arab Emirates

South Asia

Jama Masjid, Old Delhi

Shah Jahan’s Friday mosque — Delhi’s living Islamic tourist mega while Haram sites stay deferred. Visitor entry managed on-site; reference Delhi Tourism (delhitourism.gov.in) / Incredible India. No reliable single national e-ticket as of research — plan on-site payment + ID. Friday midday congregational prayer typically closes non-worshipper tourist entry. Modest dress; head covering for women often required. Do not invent INR; do not sell third-party skip-the-line as required entry.

Middle East

Imam Mosque / Shah Mosque (مسجد امام), Naqsh-e Jahan, Isfahan

UNESCO Meidan Emam Safavid congregational masterpiece on Naqsh-e Jahan’s south side. Square free; mosque separate heritage ticket (Iranian vs foreign tiers). Friday / mourning / iʿtikāf may close tourist entry. Sheikh Lotfollah + Ali Qapu are separate products. Foreign cards often fail — cash IRR honesty. Do not invent IRR/USD.

Islamic · Iran

Africa

Mosque-Madrasa of Sultan Hassan (مسجد ومدرسة السلطان حسن), Cairo

Cairo’s Mamluk mosque-madrasa masterpiece opposite the Citadel. Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities egymonuments.gov.eg — ticket commonly bundles Al-Rifa’i when published. Citadel / Mohammed Ali Mosque are separate. Foreign / student / Egyptian tiers; do not invent EGP. Modest dress; shoes off where posted. Prefer Ministry desk / official e-booking over street scalpers.

Middle East

Registan Ensemble (Регистон), Samarkand

UNESCO Samarkand three-madrasa square — Ulugh Beg, Sher-Dor, and Tilya-Kori under the Registan Directorate. Official info registon.uz; e-tickets tickets.registon.uz (Uzbek citizen vs foreign tiers). Single-entry honesty (leaving + returning usually needs a new ticket). Do not invent UZS; Bibi-Khanym and Shah-i-Zinda are separate products.

Islamic · Uzbekistan

Middle East

Sheikh Lotfollah Mosque (مسجد شیخ لطف‌الله), Naqsh-e Jahan, Isfahan

UNESCO Meidan Emam Safavid private mosque on Naqsh-e Jahan’s east side — reverse-dome masterpiece separate from live Imam Mosque. Square free; mosque separate heritage-desk ticket (Iranian vs foreign tiers). Friday / mourning / prayer closures for tourist entry. Cash IRR honesty — foreign cards often fail. Do not invent IRR/USD or “one Meidan ticket.”

Mosque of Ahmad Ibn Tulun (مسجد أحمد بن طولون), Cairo

Oldest surviving intact mosque fabric in Cairo — Tulunid spiral-minaret mega at al-Qata'i. MOTA / egymonuments.gov.eg lists visitor hours with free admission — do not invent EGP ticket. Prayer-time soft closures; Gayer-Anderson Museum next door is a separate paid MOTA product. Prefer ministry pages over OTA skip-the-line.

Islamic · Egypt

Middle East

Süleymaniye Mosque (Süleymaniye Camii), Istanbul

Mimar Sinan's Ottoman imperial masterpiece above the Golden Horn — working congregational mosque with free entry and five-prayer + Friday noon tourist closures of the prayer hall. Earns keep vs live Hagia Sophia + Blue Mosque as a distinct Sinan külliye. Do not invent TRY tickets or third-party skip-the-line; verify Diyanet / mosque notices / kulturportali.gov.tr.

Al-Azhar Mosque (الجامع الأزهر), Cairo

Fatimid foundation and living scholarly heart of Al-Azhar — Islamic Cairo's university-mosque mega. Active congregational mosque with commonly free visitor entry (not MOTA-ticketed) — do not invent EGP. Prayer + Friday noon soft closures; verify azhar.eg / local notices. Pair Ibn Tulun / Sultan Hassan on foot without inventing one Islamic Cairo ticket.

Selimiye Mosque (Selimiye Camii), Edirne

Mimar Sinan's self-declared masterpiece — UNESCO Selimiye in Edirne (not Istanbul). Free working mosque; prayer + Friday noon hall closures. Külliye museums are separate paid products when open. Earn keep vs Hagia Sophia / Blue Mosque / Süleymaniye by stressing Edirne location. Do not invent TRY mosque ticket.

Muscat capital marble mosque postcard — carpet + chandelier mega (opens Oman). Non-Muslim window Sat–Thu morning only (official mosque ~08:30–11:00); Friday closed. Experience Oman lists OMR admission — re-verify; do not invent OMR. Dress/hair cover required.
