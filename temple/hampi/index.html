Overview

UNESCO Vijayanagara sacred capital — living Virupaksha axis + Vitthala / riverside landscape. ASI Group of Monuments ticket (+ often ADA local fees); Virupaksha darshan separate. Sunrise–sunset ASI zones. Do not invent INR; multi-day site.

Hampi on the Tungabhadra is the UNESCO Group of Monuments of the Vijayanagara sacred capital — living Virupaksha temple and bazaar axis, Vitthala complex, Hemakuta, and riverside sacred landscape beyond a single-shrine page.

Frame the visit as Vijayanagara sacred urbanism, not a boulder park. Shoes off in Virupaksha sanctum zones; modest dress; no climbing closed mandapas or marked-off boulders.

Official tickets: ASI Group of Monuments (+ often ADA local toll/fee stack at checkpoints) — info asi.nic.in / Hampi Circle; e-tickets when live on asi.paygov.org.in. Virupaksha may have separate camera / darshan products from the living temple administration. Do not invent INR; state ASI zone ≠ temple darshan clearly.

ASI monuments generally sunrise–sunset; living Virupaksha commonly morning + evening darshan windows — verify temple notices + asi.nic.in. Oct–Feb preferred; Apr–May extreme heat.

Soft blockers: two-authority fee stack; heat and distance between Vitthala / Royal Centre / Hemakuta; monkey food theft; monsoon river levels; do not invent “climb Matanga for sunrise” when closures are posted.
