Overview

UNESCO Nasrid palace-city above Granada — Alcazaba, timed Nasrid Palaces, Partal, Generalife. Official Patronato tickets only (tickets.alhambra-patronato.es). Nominative ID required. Do not invent EUR; avoid third-party Nasrid scalpers.

The Alhambra and Generalife above Granada are Spain’s highest-demand Islamic heritage visit after Mezquita framing — a UNESCO Nasrid palace-city of Alcazaba, timed Nasrid Palaces, Partal gardens, and the Generalife summer estate.

Frame the visit as Nasrid court culture and Andalusi sacred-civic landscape, not a film set. Quiet in Mexuar and Court of the Lions; no climbing walls; one pass through delimited zones.

Official tickets: Patronato de la Alhambra y Generalife only — tickets.alhambra-patronato.es (info alhambra-patronato.es). Tickets are nominative; original DNI/passport required with QR. Do not invent EUR fares. Hard-warn third-party “skip line / guaranteed Nasrid” scalpers.

Day visit commonly 08:30–18:00 (15 Oct–31 Mar) / 08:30–20:00 (1 Apr–14 Oct) — verify alhambra-patronato.es. Nasrid Palaces = exact ticket time. Night and Gardens-only products exist separately. Alcazaba itinerary can change for conservation — recheck live notices.

Soft blockers: Nasrid slots sell out weeks ahead; under-12s need their own ticket even when free-tier; collect or have QR ready well before Nasrid entry. Shoulder seasons beat July heat and coach waves.
