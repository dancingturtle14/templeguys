Overview

UNESCO rock-cut Saiva mega on Mumbai harbour — Mahesa-murti Cave 1 icon. ASI e-tickets asi.paygov.org.in (Mumbai Circle to Elephanta); Gateway of India ferry is a separate Maharashtra Maritime Board / counter product. Commonly closed Monday; monsoon ferry cancellation honesty. Do not invent INR; ASI is not the ferry.

Elephanta Caves (Gharapuri) are Mumbai harbour's UNESCO rock-cut Saiva mega — the Mahesa-murti (Trimurti) of Cave 1 is the global draw, but the island group is more than one chamber.

Frame the visit as Konkan rock-cut Saivism + harbour-island honesty — not Mumbai boat picnic only. Modest dress; no climbing or touching reliefs; flash rules as posted.

Official tickets: ASI Indian Heritage e-tickets — asi.paygov.org.in (Mumbai Circle to Elephanta Caves). Indian / SAARC-BIMSTEC / foreign tiers; under-15 free when published. The ferry from Gateway of India / Apollo Bunder (or Belapur Jetty from Navi Mumbai) is separate — Gateway counter / Maharashtra Maritime Board Belapur booking (mahammb.maharashtra.gov.in). Do not invent INR; do not sell OTA Elephanta combo as an ASI product without verify; state ASI is not the ferry.

ASI commonly 09:00–17:00, closed Monday — verify asi.nic.in World Heritage Elephanta page. First boats ~09:00; last outbound mid-afternoon — leave a return-ferry buffer. Monsoon cancellations are real.

Soft blockers: Monday close; monsoon ferries; long stair heat; Cave 1 is not the whole island group myth; do not invent Ellora day-trip same ticket. SanctaWay is informational — it does not sell tickets or grant access.
