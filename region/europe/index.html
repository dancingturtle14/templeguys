Pantheon (Santa Maria ad Martyres), Rome

Delphi Archaeological Site & Museum, Phocis

UNESCO pan-Hellenic sanctuary of Apollo — oracle / navel of the Earth plus museum. Official TAP e-tickets: etickets.tap.gr / hhticket.gr (combined site + museum common). Timed museum slots when published. Do not invent EUR; warn scalper skip-line CTAs.

Sanctuary of Our Lady of Lourdes

Global Marian pilgrimage capital — Grotto of Massabielle, baths, processions. Sanctuary entry free (lourdes-france.com); paid museum/cinema add-ons optional. Pastoral ministries ≠ tourist attractions. Do not invent EUR or medical-cure claims.

Alhambra and Generalife

UNESCO Nasrid palace-city above Granada — Alcazaba, timed Nasrid Palaces, Partal, Generalife. Official Patronato tickets only (tickets.alhambra-patronato.es). Nominative ID required. Do not invent EUR; avoid third-party Nasrid scalpers.

Florence Cathedral (Santa Maria del Fiore)

Brunelleschi dome mega — Cathedral floor free; paid Opera Passes (Brunelleschi / Giotto / Ghiberti) for Dome, Bell Tower, Baptistery, Museum, Santa Reparata. Official tickets.duomo.firenze.it only. Do not invent EUR; Dome = many steps / no lift.

Sanctuary of Our Lady of Fátima

Global Marian pilgrimage capital with Lourdes — Chapel of the Apparitions, Rosary Basilica, Basilica of the Holy Trinity. Sanctuary entry free (fatima.pt). Optional museum/exhibitions paid. Do not invent EUR or miracle claims. 13th-of-month peaks May–Oct.

St Mark's Basilica (Basilica Cattedrale Patriarcale di San Marco)

Venice mosaic patriarchal cathedral — nominative timed Procuratoria tickets (tickets.basilicasanmarco.it). Basilica / Pala d’Oro / Museo-Loggia / Campanile are separate SKUs. Do not invent EUR; avoid non-Procuratoria skip-line scalpers.

Santa Maria de Montserrat Abbey

Catalan Black Madonna mountain abbey — free liturgy/Cambril reservation channels for eligible residents (abadiamontserrat.cat / reserves); non-resident tourist sanctuary entry + museum via montserratvisita.com. Cremallera/Aeri are transport, not abbey entry. Do not invent EUR or miracles.

Europe

Canterbury Cathedral

Mother church of the Anglican Communion / Becket pilgrimage see — sightseeing tickets on canterbury-cathedral.org; worship and chapel prayer free. Sunday split access. Do not invent GBP; prefer Dean & Chapter official shop over resellers.

Europe

Papal Basilica of Saint Francis, Assisi

UNESCO Assisi Franciscan pilgrimage basilica (Lower / Upper / Tomb) — individual entry free; liturgy pauses tourist flow. Official hours / visits on basilica.sanfrancesco.org. Guided visits via basilica channels. Do not invent EUR gate fares; no third-party “skip-line basilica tickets” as required entry.

Europe

St Paul's Cathedral, London

Wren’s City cathedral + Dome Galleries — official sightseeing tickets on stpauls.co.uk. Worship / prayer entry free when attending services. Sunday normally closed for sightseeing (seasonal Dome Sundays when published). Do not invent GBP; warn non-cathedral resellers.

Europe

Cathedral of Saint Mary of the See + Giralda, Seville

Largest Gothic cathedral + Giralda + Columbus tomb. Official Cabildo tickets via catedraldesevilla.es / e-tickets catedraldesevilla.servitickets.es. General visit usually bundles Cathedral + Giralda (+ Iglesia del Salvador when included). Sunday cultural visit afternoon-class. Worship ≠ sightseeing. Do not invent EUR; prefer official Serviticket.

Europe

Basilica of Saint Anthony, Padua

Major Antonian pilgrimage shrine (tomb / relics). Basilica entry free for individual visitors — santantonio.org. Paid museum single ticket covers listed museum spaces when open (Tue–Sun class). Liturgy first; feast of St Anthony (13 Jun) peak. Do not invent EUR gate fares; do not sell third-party “Padua basilica tickets” as required entry.

Christian · Italy

Europe

York Minster, York

Largest Gothic cathedral in Northern Europe — remaining UK Christian mega after live Canterbury, Westminster, and St Paul’s. Official store tickets.yorkminster.org. General admission typically bundles floor + Chapter House + Undercroft + Crypt; tower is a separate / add-on when open. Sunday sightseeing window shorter. Do not invent GBP; prefer official store over third-party skip-the-line.

Christian · United Kingdom

Europe

Mosteiro dos Jerónimos, Lisbon (Belém)

UNESCO Manueline monastery and Discoveries-era sacred complex in Belém — Portugal’s heritage crown beyond live Fátima. Cloister tickets via Museus e Monumentos de Portugal / official MEO Blueticket (mmp.bymeoblueticket.pt). Church (Santa Maria de Belém) has separate liturgy / visit windows from the paid cloister. Closed Mondays. Do not invent EUR; do not treat random OTAs as official MMP.

Christian · Portugal

Europe

St Basil's Cathedral (Pokrovsky / Cathedral of Vasily the Blessed), Red Square, Moscow

Onion-dome Russian Orthodox votive cathedral on Red Square — UNESCO Kremlin & Red Square global icon, run as a museum-cathedral by the State Historical Museum. Official tickets: tickets.shm.ru / en.shm.ru/museum/hvb/. Red Square access ≠ church-interior ticket; Kremlin Armoury / Cathedral Square are separate products. Foreign/domestic tiers differ — verify live; do not invent RUB. Museum visit ≠ liturgy-as-sightseeing.

Europe

Jasna Góra Monastery (Black Madonna), Częstochowa

Poland’s national Marian shrine — Pauline Fathers’ Jasna Góra and the Chapel of the Miraculous Image (Black Madonna). Grounds / basilica / chapel generally free; museum / treasury / tower / guided groups are separate paid via JCI cennik (jci.jasnagora.pl / jasnagora.pl). Do not invent PLN; do not sell “skip-the-line into the icon.” Mass / confession ≠ tourist show.
