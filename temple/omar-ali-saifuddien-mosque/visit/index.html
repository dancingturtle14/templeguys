Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien Mosque / Masjid Omar Ali Saifuddien, Bandar Seri Begawan

Jalan McArthur lagoon precinct, Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei

Quick facts

Founded
Completed 1958; named for Sultan Omar Ali Saifuddien III; Mughal-influenced capital mosque with artificial lagoon
Visit length
45–90 minutes hall (if open) + lagoon exterior; longer with waterfront walk
UNESCO
Not a standalone World Heritage listing (national capital landmark)
Patron / era
Islamic — national landmark mosque of Brunei Darussalam

Visit

Free — conflicting non-Muslim windows; verify locally; lagoon exterior plan-B.

Entry

Free entry / donation

Free; conflicting visitor windows; Friday/prayer pauses; lagoon exterior plan-B; no invent BND.

Hours

Non-Muslim visitor windows are limited and sources conflict. A common tourism pattern cites Mon–Wed & Sat–Sun ~08:30–12:00, ~13:30–15:00, ~16:30–17:30; Thursday often late-only; Friday often closed to visitors or late-only (~16:30–17:30 class). Prayer times, Ramadan, and events suspend access. Re-verify Brunei tourism / mosque notices / hotel desk the morning of your visit — do not treat any blog timetable as final. Golden-hour lagoon exterior is always available from the shore.

Tickets

Entry generally free — do not invent BND. Prefer official / tourism-board notices over cruise “VIP” markups. No invent combo covering Jame’ Asr as one pass.

Dress

Active grand mosque — covered arms/legs; women hair cover; robes often provided at the entrance; shoes off; quiet; no flash photography of people in prayer; follow staff lanes in the visitor area.

Duration

45–90 minutes if hall open; allow lagoon exterior time either way.

Access

Bandar Seri Begawan centre — Jalan McArthur lagoon. Walk / taxi from Yayasan or Kampong Ayer waterfront. Interior access only in visitor windows; exterior always. Not one ticket with Jame’ Asr.

Tips

  • Confirm non-Muslim hours the morning of your visit — published sources conflict.
  • Treat Friday and prayer times as closed or heavily restricted for non-Muslim visitors.
  • If the hall is closed, walk the lagoon edge for the postcard view — still a complete visit plan.
  • Use lent robes / hair cover; remove shoes; stay in marked visitor areas.
  • Do not invent BND fees or a Jame’ Asr combo ticket.
  • Pair Royal Regalia Museum as a separate civic stop.
  • SanctaWay does not sell tickets or grant access.
