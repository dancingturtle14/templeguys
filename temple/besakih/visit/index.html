Pura Agung Besakih

Besakih, Karangasem, Bali

Quick facts

Founded
Balinese Hindu mother temple complex on the slopes of Gunung Agung; multi-pura terraced sanctuary
Visit length
Half day from south Bali including road time; longer if ceremony viewing from allowed zones
UNESCO
Not individually inscribed as a World Heritage property — Bali’s paramount living Hindu temple complex
Patron / era
Hindu (Balinese Agama Hindu Dharma) — Pura Agung Besakih

Hours

Visitor windows commonly daylight (~07:00–18:00 class) — verify besakih.org and local notices. Major ceremonies can restrict tourist routes without long notice — recheck before travel.

Tickets

Buy at the official ticket office following besakih.org guidance and Bali provincial visitor rules. Foreign tickets often include sarong/sash, local guide, and shuttle when operating. Do not invent IDR. Hard-warn roadside touts before the real gate.

Dress

Living Balinese Hindu Mother Temple — sarong and sash required; shoulders covered. Menstruating visitors traditionally asked not to enter inner sacred zones — follow staff guidance respectfully. No entering closed jero sanctums.

Duration

2–3 hours on the terraces plus 1.5–2.5 hr road each way from south Bali depending on base and traffic.

Access

Drive / private driver from south Bali up toward Gunung Agung; park at designated Manik Mas / official lots — not random roadside touts. Electric shuttle / walk up terrace levels when operating. Stairs and heat matter.

Tips

  • Buy only at the official counter — ignore roadside ticket+guide touts.
  • Sarong and sash are required; they are often bundled in the visitor product.
  • Recheck ceremony / karya notices — tourist routes can close.
  • Follow menstrual-access guidance for inner zones without argument or stigma.
  • Watch Gunung Agung volcanic advisories when active.
  • Stay on public visitor paths; do not enter pemedek-only courtyards.
  • Bring water and sun protection for the terraces.
