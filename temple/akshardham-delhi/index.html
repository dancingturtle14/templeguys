Overview

One of India's highest-throughput sacred complexes — BAPS Swaminarayan Akshardham, New Delhi. Mandir darshan free (no appointment); exhibitions / water show paid on-site per akshardham.com. Closed Monday; large security screening; no large bags. Do not invent INR; not the Gandhinagar campus.

Swaminarayan Akshardham in New Delhi is one of India's highest-throughput sacred complexes — a BAPS mandir campus where free darshan sits beside paid exhibitions and a seasonal water show.

Frame the visit as BAPS devotion + contemporary Hindu monumental complex honesty — not theme-park India only. Modest dress; no meat, alcohol, or tobacco on campus; phone and camera rules as posted for mandir vs exhibitions.

Official tickets: mandir complex entry and darshan are free (no appointment). Exhibitions and water show are paid on-site counters per akshardham.com (published adult / senior / child exhibition tiers — do not invent INR as frozen forever; verify live). Groups of 50+ use the advance request page. Prefer the official site over scalpers. Soft-warn: this is not Gandhinagar Akshardham (different campus).

Complex commonly Tue–Sun, first entry ~10:00 / last entry ~18:30; closed Monday — verify akshardham.com/visitor-info/. Mandir darshan windows can run longer than exhibition ticket windows; water show after sunset when running.

Soft blockers: Monday close; bag and security rules (no large bags — vehicle leave / cloak rules per site); exhibition queue is not free darshan; water-show seasonal / slot honesty; do not invent online skip-the-line mandir ticket (darshan is free). SanctaWay is informational — it does not sell tickets or grant access.
