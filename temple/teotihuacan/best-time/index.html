Best time

Opening hour; verify climb policy; Sundays denser for nationals.

Earliest entry is the best comfort window on the Avenue. Dry-season heat and equinox operativos need extra planning — INAH may restrict climbs and close museums on peak ritual-tourism days.

Sunday free-entry rules for Mexican nationals increase density. If you need quieter plazas, choose a weekday opening slot.

  • Open at 08:00 when possible
  • Weekdays quieter than Sunday national free entry
  • Equinox / special operativos: read INAH notices
  • Never assume pyramid climbs are open
