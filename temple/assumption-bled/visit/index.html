Tickets

Church often free / donation when open. Boat / pletna fares are separate lakeside products — do not invent EUR. Soft primary https://www.bled.si/en/what-to-see/bled-island/ (+ slovenia.info). Prefer destination Soft over OTA island packages. Not one ticket with Bled Castle or every Alps church.

Duration

Access

Pletna / boat from Bled lakeside piers; walk/cycle lakeside from town. Ljubljana longer gateway. Weather and weddings reshape access.
