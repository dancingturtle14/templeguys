Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺) / Feilai Feng Scenic, Hangzhou

West Lake / Feilai Feng corridor, Hangzhou

Trad. Eastern Jin / Hui Li founding legend; successive rebuilds; Feilai Feng grotto continuum

Reserved for partners — not live yet

Visit

Free scenic with mandatory real-name time-slot — not a paid gate myth.

Entry

Free entry

Free + real-name reserve; do not invent CNY; on-site registration suspended 2026-02.

Lingyin / Feilai Feng — lingyinsi.org + mini-program 杭州灵隐飞来峰 →

Hours

Scenic commonly ~07:30–17:30 (entry ends ~17:00) — verify lingyinsi.org / 杭州灵隐飞来峰 mini-program. Morning and afternoon slots; morning cooler.

Tickets

Admission-free real-name time-slot via WeChat/Alipay mini-program 杭州灵隐飞来峰 (advance; same-day often blocked). On-site registration suspended from 2026-02-01 — join online waitlist. Original ID for gate. Hotline 0571-87969691. Do not invent CNY; do not buy third-party “skip-the-line” as entry.

Dress

Living Chan monastery inside West Lake scenic — shoulders/knees covered in halls; quiet at Mahavira Hall; no climbing Feilai Feng carvings.

Duration

Half day for Feilai Feng + Lingyin; add buffer for hillside paths and Yongfu / Taoguang if open on your slot.

Access

Hangzhou West Lake / Feilai Feng corridor; bus / metro + walk / taxi to Lingyin Feilai Peak gate. Not a city-center metro-exit temple — plan transfer time.

Tips

  • Reserve via 杭州灵隐飞来峰 at least one day ahead — verify live.
  • Do not invent CNY; scenic is free with real-name slot.
  • On-site registration suspended from 2026-02-01 — use waitlist.
  • Bring original passport/ID for gate verification.
  • No-show credit penalties apply — cancel before the posted cutoff.
  • Do not climb or touch Feilai Feng carvings.
  • Rain makes hillside paths slippery — wear grip shoes.
