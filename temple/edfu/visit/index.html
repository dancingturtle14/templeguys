Temple of Horus at Edfu (معبد إدفو), Aswan Governorate

Edfu town, Nile west bank between Luxor and Aswan

Quick facts

Founded
Ptolemaic Temple of Horus (construction begun under Ptolemy III; completed under later Ptolemies)
Visit length
90–120 minutes temple circuit; Nile cruise calèche / taxi from dock common
UNESCO
Part of the broader Egyptian monumental landscape; ticketed MOTA monument (verify egymonuments.gov.eg / egymonuments.com)
Patron / era
Ancient Egyptian — Horus of Edfu (Ptolemaic temple; mammisi / birth-house fabric)

Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

MOTA daytime window — verify egymonuments.gov.eg / egymonuments.com (last-entry vs close as published). Morning before cruise groups.

Tickets

MOTA e-tickets — egymonuments.com Temple of Horus at Edfu. Foreign / student / Egyptian tiers — do not invent EGP. Prefer MOTA over OTA. Kom Ombo / Dendara / Luxor temples / Valley of the Kings = separate products.

Dress

Ptolemaic Temple of Horus — modest practical dress; no climbing pylons off-path; no touching reliefs; sanctuary photo rules as posted.

Duration

90–120 minutes temple circuit; mammisi included when open on the circuit.

Access

Nile cruise mid-stop between Luxor and Aswan; or road from Luxor (~2 h class) / Aswan. Town calèche / taxi from dock to temple common — negotiate calmly; temple ticket is separate.

Tips

  • Buy MOTA e-ticket on egymonuments.com — do not invent EGP.
  • Do not invent one Nile temple pass with Luxor / Philae / Abu Simbel.
  • Morning beats cruise group crush and midday heat.
  • Calèche from dock is separate commercial — temple ticket is MOTA.
  • No climbing pylons off-path; no touching reliefs.
  • Kom Ombo and Dendara stay separate tickets (B20+ densify).
  • Prefer MOTA over random OTA “Edfu VIP.”
