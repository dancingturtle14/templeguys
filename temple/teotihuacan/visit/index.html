Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Commonly 08:00–17:00 (last entry ~16:30) — verify visitateotihuacan.inah.gob.mx / INAH listings. Special operativos (e.g. equinox) can alter rules and museum hours.

Tickets

INAH official channels — ventadeboletosenlinea.inah.gob.mx for e-boletos; visit info visitateotihuacan.inah.gob.mx. Museums typically bundled when open — confirm live. Nationality / Sunday free-entry rules for Mexican nationals and residents with ID apply when posted. Do not invent MXN fares.

Dress

Sun-protective clothing, hat, and broken-in walking shoes. Little shade on the Avenue. Layers for cooler mornings and hot plazas.

Duration

3–5 hours for Avenue highlights plus museum time. Do not plan a climb-centric itinerary — access to pyramid tops is often closed or limited.

Access

From Mexico City: bus from Central del Norte, authorized taxi, or organized day tour to the gates. On-site: walk the Avenue between Moon Plaza, Sun Pyramid, and Ciudadela. Stay on authorized paths. Drones without permit are prohibited. Heat and sun exposure are the main physical constraints.

Tips

  • Buy via INAH e-boletos / official taquillas — not random resellers.
  • Re-check INAH climbing notices the week you visit — Sun/Moon access changes.
  • Start at opening for cooler air and thinner Avenue crowds.
  • Carry water and sun protection — shade is scarce.
  • Sunday free national entry increases density — plan accordingly.
  • Museum time is worth scheduling when galleries are open.
  • No drones; stay off closed basements and cordoned climbs.
