Southeast Asia

Borobudur

A vast 9th-century Mahayana Buddhist stupa-temple on Java — timed structure tickets via official Borobudur park channels, special sandals on the stone, and strict daily quotas.

Buddhist · Indonesia

Read visit guide →

East Asia

Meiji Shrine

A forest Shinto shrine in central Tokyo dedicated to Emperor Meiji and Empress Shōken — free precincts from sunrise to sunset, with paid Inner Garden and museum options.

Southeast Asia

Wat Arun

Bangkok’s Temple of Dawn on the Thonburi bank — porcelain prang, steep terrace stairs, and a short river ferry from Tha Tien near Wat Pho.

South Asia

Harmandir Sahib (Golden Temple)

Sikhism’s holiest gurdwara in Amritsar — gold-clad Harmandir Sahib in the Amrit Sarovar, free to all who cover their heads and remove shoes.

Sikh · India

East Asia

Po Lin Monastery & Tian Tan Buddha

Hong Kong’s Ngong Ping monastery and Tian Tan Buddha — cable-car country, free monastery grounds, and weather-dependent Big Buddha access above Lantau.

Buddhist · Hong Kong

Middle East

Hagia Sophia

Justinian’s great church, now Ayasofya Grand Mosque — ticketed upper-gallery visitor route for tourists, free worship access, facing the Blue Mosque in Sultanahmet.

Islamic · Christian · Türkiye
