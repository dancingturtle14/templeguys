Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Commonly 08:00–18:00 Mar–Oct and 08:00–17:00 Nov–Feb; last admission earlier — verify lmsk.cn / en.lmsk.cn. Night products when published for west-hill subsets.

Tickets

Official: lmsk.cn / en.lmsk.cn and official WeChat booking channels. Real-name ticketing common — passport/ID must match. Do not invent CNY. Prefer official channels over street scalpers. Combined west/east hill products when published — verify inclusions.

Dress

Buddhist cave-temple UNESCO site — comfortable walking shoes for stairs; sun protection in summer. Respectful quiet at major niches.

Duration

3–5 hours for west hill highlights; full day if adding east hill / Xiangshan Temple axis honestly.

Access

Fly/rail into Luoyang; city bus / taxi / tourist bus to Longmen scenic area. West and east hills face across the Yi River — plan walking time. Stairs and summer sun are the main physical limits.

Tips

  • Book via lmsk.cn / official WeChat — ignore street scalpers.
  • Bring passport/ID matching the real-name ticket.
  • Budget separate time for west vs east hills.
  • No touching statues; no unauthorised drones/tripods.
  • Shaolin is a different day trip — not the same ticket.
  • Spring/autumn preferred for heat and haze.
  • Offline map helps — Mandarin-first signage common.
