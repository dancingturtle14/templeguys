South Asia

Swayambhunath Stupa (Monkey Temple), Kathmandu

Kathmandu Valley UNESCO hill stupa — completes the valley triangle with live Pashupatinath + Boudhanath. On-site heritage entry; nationality tiers via Nepal Tourism Board (ntb.gov.np). Cash NPR common; no reliable official advance e-ticket. Walk clockwise; do not feed monkeys. Do not invent NPR.
