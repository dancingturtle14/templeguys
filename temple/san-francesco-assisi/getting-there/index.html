Getting there

How visitors usually reach the basilica in Assisi.

Train to Assisi or Santa Maria degli Angeli, then bus, taxi, or a steep walk up to Piazza San Francesco.

Lower Church, Upper Church, Tomb, and Sacred Convent share one campus — slot liturgy vs tourist flow when you plan.

Assisi hill slopes and stairs are real; build buffer if combining with other Assisi churches.

  • — Train → Assisi / Santa Maria degli Angeli
  • — Bus/taxi/walk up to Piazza San Francesco
  • — Lower / Upper / Tomb = one campus, different hours
  • — Hill slopes / stairs — pace yourself
