Hours

Abbey: typically 9:00–19:00 (1 May–31 Aug) and 9:30–18:00 (1 Sep–30 Apr); closed 1 Jan, 1 May, 25 Dec. Last entry ~1 hour before closing. Village accessible without ticket hours. Verify https://www.abbaye-mont-saint-michel.fr/en/visit/practical-information before travel.

Tickets

Abbey tickets via Centre des monuments nationaux / abbey channels. Published individual rates for 2026 listings include about €16 (Apr–Sep) and €13 (Oct–Mar) with free entry for under-18s (family rules apply) and many 18–25 EU citizens — check eligibility documents. First Sundays of some winter months and European Heritage Days may be free. Parking is paid separately on the mainland (montsaintmichel.gouv.fr).

Dress

Comfortable walking shoes with grip for polished stone and steep lanes. Weather layers — the bay is windy and can be cold even in summer. Modest clothing inside abbey churches/chapels.

Duration

4–7 hours door-to-door including parking, shuttle, climb, abbey, and return; abbey interior alone often 1.5–2 hours.

Access

Long stair sequences to the abbey; not a flat site. Some assisted options exist but the historic core remains demanding — research PMR information on official sites if mobility is limited. Expect crowds on Grande Rue; side ramparts can be slightly calmer.

Tips

  • Reserve mainland parking online in peak season via official MSM parking channels.
  • Take Le Passeur shuttle; walking the bridge is possible but exposed.
  • Start early to climb before cruise-coach waves.
  • Buy abbey tickets from official CMN/abbey sources — not random apps claiming ‘Mont entry’.
  • Carry water; village restaurants are pricey and queues are long at lunch.
  • Check tide/weather if considering any bay walk — only with accredited guides.
  • Last abbey entry is earlier than closing — do not arrive at the ticket desk at closing time.
