Overview

Fatimid foundation and living scholarly heart of Al-Azhar — Islamic Cairo's university-mosque mega. Active congregational mosque with commonly free visitor entry (not MOTA-ticketed) — do not invent EGP. Prayer + Friday noon soft closures; verify azhar.eg / local notices. Pair Ibn Tulun / Sultan Hassan on foot without inventing one Islamic Cairo ticket.

Al-Azhar Mosque is Islamic Cairo's Fatimid foundation mega and the living scholarly heart tied to Al-Azhar — still an active university-mosque rather than a thin Khan el-Khalili photo stop.

Frame the visit as Fatimid foundation + living Al-Azhar scholarly institution honesty. Active congregational etiquette: shoulders and knees covered; remove shoes; women head covering customary (loan often available); quiet during prayer; do not cut through salah lines.

Official tickets: admission is commonly free — Al-Azhar is not sold as a MOTA antiquities ticket. Shoe-keep tips are customary soft costs — do not invent an EGP ticket. Prefer mosque / Al-Azhar portals (azhar.eg class) and local notices over OTA Al-Azhar skip-the-line products.

Tourist entry is commonly daytime between prayers; Friday noon is crowded and often unsuitable for sightseeing. There is no reliable MOTA fixed tourist timetable — verify azhar.eg notices and on-site guidance. Mid-morning is usually the calmest class.

Soft blockers: prayer and Friday closures; free ≠ always-open museum; access for non-Muslim visitors is practice-dependent when the courtyard is open for visitors — state as practice, not a guarantee; do not invent one Islamic Cairo combo covering Ibn Tulun + Sultan Hassan + Al-Azhar. SanctaWay is informational — it does not sell tickets or grant access.
