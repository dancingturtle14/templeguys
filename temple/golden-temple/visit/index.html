Visit

Free, open, and busy. Bring a head cover and leave intoxicants behind.

Entry

Free entry

Entry is free. Follow on-site sevadar guidance for queues, photography, and langar.

District Amritsar — Sri Harmandir Sahib overview →

Hours

Complex access is effectively from early morning into late night (commonly cited windows such as roughly 4:00am–10:00pm for general visiting patterns) with religious life continuing around the clock — verify locally. Langar operates 24 hours.

Tickets

No admission ticket. Donations are voluntary. Ignore anyone selling “VIP Golden Temple entry.”

Dress

Modest clothing covering shoulders and knees. Head covering required for all genders inside the complex (scarves provided). Remove shoes; wash feet at the entrance pools/channels.

Duration

2–3 hours for parikrama and sanctum queue; longer if you serve or eat in langar.

Access

Marble parikrama can be slippery when wet; steps into the sanctum causeway. Wheelchairs: ask sevadars for assisted routes — crowds are the main obstacle. Shoe facility and head-cover stands at entrances.

Tips

  • Carry your own light scarf; free covers exist but peak times run out of neat ones.
  • Use official shoe service; keep the token safe.
  • Early morning or evening light on the sarovar is calmer than midday heat.
  • Join langar with clean hands and follow seating directions — it is worshipful service, not a food court.
  • No cigarettes, e-cigs, alcohol, or intoxicants anywhere in the complex.
  • Store large luggage at your hotel; big bags complicate security.
  • From Amritsar Junction ~2 km by auto; airport ATQ ~13 km by prepaid taxi.
