Santiago de Compostela, Galicia, Spain

Visit

Free basilica for prayer and respectful visit; pay only for official museum / Portico / roof products.

Entry

Free entry

Basilica/tomb typically free. Paid museum/Portico/roof products only via official booking. Confirm live rates — no invented fares.

Catedral de Santiago — official visits booking →

Hours

Basilica and Apostle’s tomb: typically long daily hours — verify catedraldesantiago.es. Museum spaces: shorter daytime windows. Pilgrim’s Mass schedule is separate. Avoid circulating as a tourist group during liturgy.

Tickets

Basilica / tomb: typically free. Museum, Portico of Glory, roofs / tower, night visits: book via visitas.catedraldesantiago.es / cathedral museum channels. Confirm live € rates on official pages — do not invent packages. Compostela / Pilgrim Office is a separate process.

Dress

Modest clothing suitable for an active cathedral (shoulders covered preferred). Comfortable shoes for stone floors. Large Camino packs stay outside or in lockers.

Duration

45–90 minutes for basilica and tomb; 2–4 hours if you add museum, Portico, or rooftop visits.

Access

Stone floors, steps, and uneven historic fabric. Some assisted routes exist — check cathedral accessibility notices. Rooftop visits are guided/timed and physically demanding.

Tips

  • Use Porta de Praterías for the usual tourist entry pattern.
  • Book Portico / museum / roof slots only on official visitas.catedraldesantiago.es.
  • Do not walk tour routes during Mass.
  • Leave large backpacks in lockers — they are banned inside.
  • Do not touch the Portico of Glory.
  • Pilgrim credential / Compostela is separate from cathedral tickets.
  • Verify same-week hours around major feast days.
