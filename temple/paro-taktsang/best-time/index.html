Best time

Clear mornings; treat monsoon mud as a real closer.

Dry, clear mornings give the postcard cliff view and safer footing. Cloud and rain erase the vista and make the final stairs treacherous.

Peak tourism seasons fill the trail; shoulder weeks can be kinder if your Bhutan itinerary allows flexibility. Always defer to same-day weather calls from guides.

  • Best: early clear mornings
  • Avoid late starts that miss ticket windows
  • Monsoon: expect mud, fog, and possible upper closures
  • Altitude + stairs: pace yourself even if fit
