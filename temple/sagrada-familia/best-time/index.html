Best time

Weekday mornings for light and slightly thinner crowds.

Book morning slots for the famous coloured light on the nave floor. Weekends and summer evenings are popular; Sundays open later and coincide with worship patterns.

Construction noise and cranes are part of the visit — not a defect. If you want quieter audio-guide focus, avoid peak holiday weeks when possible.

  • Best: weekday morning timed entry
  • Golden light: mornings through the stained glass
  • Summer: hydrate before security
  • Recheck tower status after high winds
