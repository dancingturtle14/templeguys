Bayon Temple, Angkor Thom, Siem Reap

Angkor Thom, Siem Reap

Quick facts

Founded
Late 12th / early 13th c. Mahayana state temple of Jayavarman VII; later Hinduized alterations; Angkor Thom mandala centre
Visit length
1–2 hours on Small Circuit after Angkor Wat; multi-day pass recommended for full park
UNESCO
UNESCO World Heritage — Angkor (Angkor Archaeological Park)
Patron / era
Buddhist — Jayavarman VII Mahayana royal cult (Avalokiteshvara / face-tower cosmos); later Hindu overlays

Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Temple commonly ~07:30–17:30 class — verify APSARA / Angkor Enterprise notices. Early or late light preferred; midday harsh. Central tower may close for conservation.

Tickets

Angkor Pass only via Angkor Enterprise — angkorenterprise.gov.kh / ticket.angkorenterprise.gov.kh (web, app, kiosk, Road 60 counters). Bayon included in 1/3/7-day park pass — no separate Bayon gate ticket. Tickets bought elsewhere not valid. Koh Ker / Beng Mealea are separate products. Hard-warn scalpers. Do not invent USD.

Dress

Sacred Khmer Mahayana / later Hinduized landscape — shoulders/knees covered (Angkor Code of Conduct); no climbing restricted towers; no touching faces.

Duration

1–2 hours at Bayon on the Small Circuit; multi-day pass for fuller Angkor Thom + Grand Circuit.

Access

Siem Reap base; tuk-tuk / car on Small Circuit after Angkor Wat. Enter Angkor Thom via South Gate most commonly; Bayon sits at the city centre.

Tips

  • Buy the Angkor Pass only on angkorenterprise.gov.kh / ticket.angkorenterprise.gov.kh — same pass as Angkor Wat.
  • There is no separate Bayon ticket — ignore street sellers claiming otherwise.
  • Ask on the day whether the central tower is open for conservation.
  • Shoulders and knees covered — Angkor Code of Conduct enforced.
  • Early morning or late soft light beats midday heat on the face towers.
  • Do not conflate Bayon with separate Koh Ker / Beng Mealea tickets.
  • Do not invent USD; verify live pass prices on Angkor Enterprise.
