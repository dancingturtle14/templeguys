Getting there

How visitors usually reach Prambanan from Yogyakarta.

From Yogyakarta, common options are TransJogja / Prambanan bus services, train to Brambanan / Prambanan station, taxi, Grab, or a tour van. Road time is often about 30–45 minutes depending on traffic.

On site, expect walking between Prambanan, Lumbung, Bubrah, and Sewu. Same-day Ratu Boko combos are popular but add heat and ticket complexity — plan deliberately.

If you also visit Borobudur, treat them as two major days or a carefully paced long day with separate tickets and recovery time. Do not rely on informal roadside ticket sellers.

  • — Yogyakarta base → bus, train, taxi, or tour van (~30–45 min)
  • — Walk between Prambanan and Sewu cluster on marked paths
  • — Official park tickets only — no street resellers
  • — Budget heat, water, and Yogyakarta traffic both ways
