Great Mosque of Kairouan (Mosque of Uqba), Tunisia

UNESCO Maghreb pilgrimage mosque — living courtyard tourism with clear non-Muslim rules (courtyard typical; prayer hall generally closed to non-Muslims). Official AMVPPC: patrimoinedetunisie.com.tn. On-site ticket common. Do not invent TND; Friday/Ramadan hours shrink.

Aksum / Axum sacred cluster (Stelae Park and Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion)

Aksumite capital + Ethiopian Orthodox Zion tradition — buy on-site at official ticket offices (stelae/heritage ticket separate from Mary of Zion). Old Church often male-only; Chapel of the Tablet (claimed Ark) — no public entry. Cash ETB common. Do not invent ETB; no relic-tourist framing.

Africa

Philae Temple (Isis) / Agilkia Island, Aswan

Relocated Isis temple — UNESCO Nubian salvage icon on Agilkia Island after High Dam. Official Ministry tickets via egymonuments.gov.eg / egymonuments.com (or marina booth). Boat transfer from Marina Philae (Shellal) is mandatory and usually a separate cash payment — not included in the monument ticket. Sound & Light is a separate evening product. Do not invent EGP. Abu Simbel is a different site/day.

Mosque-Madrasa of Sultan Hassan (مسجد ومدرسة السلطان حسن), Cairo

Cairo’s Mamluk mosque-madrasa masterpiece opposite the Citadel. Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities egymonuments.gov.eg — ticket commonly bundles Al-Rifa’i when published. Citadel / Mohammed Ali Mosque are separate. Foreign / student / Egyptian tiers; do not invent EGP. Modest dress; shoes off where posted. Prefer Ministry desk / official e-booking over street scalpers.

Mosque of Ahmad Ibn Tulun (مسجد أحمد بن طولون), Cairo

Oldest surviving intact mosque fabric in Cairo — Tulunid spiral-minaret mega at al-Qata'i. MOTA / egymonuments.gov.eg lists visitor hours with free admission — do not invent EGP ticket. Prayer-time soft closures; Gayer-Anderson Museum next door is a separate paid MOTA product. Prefer ministry pages over OTA skip-the-line.

Islamic · Egypt

Al-Azhar Mosque (الجامع الأزهر), Cairo

Fatimid foundation and living scholarly heart of Al-Azhar — Islamic Cairo's university-mosque mega. Active congregational mosque with commonly free visitor entry (not MOTA-ticketed) — do not invent EGP. Prayer + Friday noon soft closures; verify azhar.eg / local notices. Pair Ibn Tulun / Sultan Hassan on foot without inventing one Islamic Cairo ticket.

Luxor Temple (معبد الأقصر), Luxor

East-bank MOTA mega — Luxor Temple (Amun of Luxor / Opet), distinct from live Karnak (~3 km north, separate ticket). Official egymonuments.gov.eg / egymonuments.com. Foreign / student / Egyptian tiers — do not invent EGP. Evening illumination popular; lighting ≠ free after-hours entry. Avenue of Sphinxes / Luxor Museum separate when ticketed.

West-bank MOTA terrace mega at Deir el-Bahari — Hatshepsut’s mortuary temple against the cliffs. Distinct from live east-bank Karnak and Luxor Temple. Official egymonuments.gov.eg / egymonuments.com — do not invent EGP. Valley of the Kings is a separate ticket/stop; midday heat soft blocker.

Best-preserved Ptolemaic Temple of Horus — every Nile cruise midpoint stop; MOTA egymonuments.com. Distinct from live Luxor cluster (Karnak / Luxor Temple / Hatshepsut) and Aswan Philae / Abu Simbel. Do not invent EGP; Kom Ombo / Dendara = separate tickets.
