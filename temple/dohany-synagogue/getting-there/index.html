Getting there

How visitors usually reach Dohány Street Synagogue in Budapest.

Most visitors use metro or tram to the Jewish Quarter and walk to Dohány utca — Astoria and Deák are common walk-in anchors.

Rumbach and Kazinczy synagogues are nearby peers with separate access rules — do not invent one free ticket covering all three interiors.

Security and capacity can reshape walk-up timing; pre-booked tickets help on peak days.

  • — Budapest Jewish Quarter / Dohány utca
  • — Metro / tram / walk from Astoria–Deák
  • — Security screening at entry
  • — No free three-synagogue wander myth
