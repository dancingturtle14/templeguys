Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Cathedral commonly daytime long hours with earlier last entry — verify duomomilano.it. Museum closed days vary (confirm live). Terrace hours follow your booked product.

Tickets

Veneranda Fabbrica del Duomo — duomomilano.it (info) and ticket.duomomilano.it (booking). Typical products: Duomo alone, Duomo+Museum, Terraces by stairs or lift, and combos. Do not invent EUR fares. Use official booking as primary CTA; third-party markup is unnecessary for standard entry.

Dress

Active cathedral dress code — cover shoulders and knees; no beachwear or bare midriffs. Quiet, respectful clothing on terraces as well.

Duration

1.5–3 hours for nave highlights; museum and terraces each add meaningful time. Combining all three needs a half-day mindset, not a 45-minute dash.

Access

Metro Duomo (lines M1/M3) to Piazza del Duomo. Security screening for cathedral / museum / terraces. Lift terrace option exists as a ticketed product for those who need it — stairs are many. Bag and ID rules follow the booking product.

Tips

  • Book on ticket.duomomilano.it — official Fabbrica channel.
  • Choose stairs vs lift terrace deliberately before you buy.
  • Confirm museum open days if that combo matters to you.
  • Cover shoulders and knees or you may be refused entry.
  • Last entry is earlier than closing — do not cut it fine.
  • Piazza pickpockets: keep bags closed and fronts-facing.
  • Verify whether your ticket type asks for ID/passport.
