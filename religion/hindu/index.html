Tirumala Venkateswara Temple, Tirupati

One of the world’s highest-volume living Hindu pilgrimage temples (TTD). Official: tirumala.org; bookings ttdevasthanams.ap.gov.in (+ official TTD app). Sarva / Special Entry Darshan / sevas / accommodation — each has rules. Do not invent INR; hard-warn fake TTD clones and WhatsApp agents.

Pura Agung Besakih

Bali’s Mother Temple on Gunung Agung — living Agama Hindu Dharma complex. Official guidance besakih.org; foreign tickets often bundle sarong/sash + local guide + shuttle. Ceremony closures possible. Do not invent IDR; hard-warn roadside touts.

Sun Temple, Konark

UNESCO Surya chariot temple — ASI Puri Circle ticketed monument (asi.nic.in / asipuricircle.in; e-ticket when live on asi.paygov.org.in). Nationality tiers; sunrise–sunset. Heritage site, not living daily sanctum darshan. Do not invent INR.

South Asia

Pashupatinath Temple, Kathmandu

Nepal’s holiest Shiva temple on the Bagmati — PADT complex. Main sanctum Hindus only; non-Hindus welcome on ghats / gardens / surrounding shrines and may observe evening aarti from the east bank. Heritage entry tiers via NTB — verify live; do not invent NPR. No sanctum photography; cremation ghat ethics.

Batu Caves (Sri Subramaniar Swamy), Selangor

272-step Murugan staircase and giant statue — KL day-trip Hindu mega. Main Temple Cave / plaza climb generally free (Sri Subramaniar Swamy Devasthanam). Optional paid caves (Ramayana Cave, Cave Villa, Dark Cave when open) sold on-site. Shoulders + knees covered enforced at stairs. Do not invent MYR; do not treat Viator as official temple entry.

Mỹ Sơn Sanctuary, Quảng Nam (near Hội An / Đà Nẵng)

UNESCO Cham Hindu temple towers in a mountain valley — Vietnam’s sacred archaeological crown. Official BQL info disanvanhoamyson.vn; e-tickets mysonticketonline.vn. Foreign / domestic / child tiers — ticket commonly bundles museum + shuttle + Cham show + ruins when published. Do not invent VND; prefer BQL / official e-ticket over random OTA as primary CTA.

South Asia

Khajuraho Group of Monuments (Western Group / Kandariya Mahadeva), Madhya Pradesh

UNESCO Chandela temple-city — Western Group (Kandariya Mahadeva and peers) is the ticketed ASI core. Official e-tickets asi.paygov.org.in (Jabalpur circle → Western Group of Temples, Khajuraho). Eastern / Southern groups commonly free. Sound-and-light is MP Tourism separate. Hard-warn fake ticket .com lookalikes. Do not invent INR.

South Asia

Brihadeeswarar / Brihadisvara Temple (பெருவுடையார் கோயில்), Thanjavur

UNESCO Great Living Chola Temples — Rajaraja I’s granite vimana mega at Thanjavur (Rājarājeśvaram / Peruvudaiyar). Living HR&CE Śaiva temple + ASI World Heritage monument. ASI lists admission free; still/handheld photo commonly free — verify asi.nic.in / hrce.tn.gov.in. Do not invent INR entry tickets; Gangaikonda Cholapuram and Darasuram are separate sibling visits.

South Asia

Group of Monuments at Mahabalipuram / Mamallapuram (Shore Temple + Pancha Rathas), Tamil Nadu

UNESCO Pallava coastal temple-city — Shore Temple, Pancha Rathas, Arjuna’s Penance and peer monuments at Mamallapuram. ASI Indian Heritage e-tickets via asi.paygov.org.in (Chennai circle → Group of Monuments, Mamallapuram). Indian / SAARC-BIMSTEC / foreign tiers; under-15 free when published. Do not invent INR; Shore ≠ unchecked whole-group myth.

South Asia

Elephanta Caves / Gharapuri (एलिफंटा गुफाएँ), Mumbai harbour

UNESCO rock-cut Saiva mega on Mumbai harbour — Mahesa-murti Cave 1 icon. ASI e-tickets asi.paygov.org.in (Mumbai Circle to Elephanta); Gateway of India ferry is a separate Maharashtra Maritime Board / counter product. Commonly closed Monday; monsoon ferry cancellation honesty. Do not invent INR; ASI is not the ferry.

Swaminarayan Akshardham (स्वामिनारायण अक्षरधाम), New Delhi

One of India's highest-throughput sacred complexes — BAPS Swaminarayan Akshardham, New Delhi. Mandir darshan free (no appointment); exhibitions / water show paid on-site per akshardham.com. Closed Monday; large security screening; no large bags. Do not invent INR; not the Gandhinagar campus.

Ramanathaswamy Temple (இராமநாதசுவாமி கோயில்), Rameswaram
