Famen Temple (法门寺), Fufeng, Shaanxi

Famen Town, Fufeng County, Baoji (Xi’an-region west), Shaanxi

Quick facts

Founded
Tang-era Famen Temple relic tradition (finger-bone / true-body pagoda fame); modern cultural scenic area with Namaste Dagoba complex
Visit length
Day trip from Xi’an (~1–1.5 h class west to Fufeng); tour bus / private car common — not inside Xi’an city-wall cluster
UNESCO
Not a standalone World Heritage listing (national 5A-class cultural scenic area)
Patron / era
Buddhist — finger-relic veneration complex; monastery + museum / scenic-area lanes

Getting there

How visitors usually reach Famen from Xi’an.

Most visitors reach Fufeng by road west from Xi’an (~1–1.5 h class) on licensed tour bus or private car. High-speed rail + local transfer patterns also exist — verify current station pairings.

Buy real-name tickets via official WeChat 法门文化景区 when required. Do not invent CNY from old posts or OTA VIP packages.

Terracotta Warriors and Big Wild Goose Pagoda are separate Xi’an-region products. Do not invent a single Shaanxi sacred pass.
