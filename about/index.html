About SanctaWay

SanctaWay is an informational visit-guide atlas for temples, churches, mosques, shrines, and sacred places worldwide. We gather practical details — hours, dress, etiquette, routes, and seasonal tips — so travellers can visit with clarity and respect.

Hours and access rules change. When something may be uncertain, we say verify before visit. We do not invent consecration claims or speak for any faith community.

This site is built for high-quality reading and browsing. Accounts and feedback tools may arrive later; v1 is open to everyone without login.

Photo credits

Each place page credits its hero photograph. Newer guides use Wikimedia Commons. Some earlier pages still carry prototype Unsplash images, named in the credit line, until they are replaced. Fallback silhouettes appear if a remote image fails to load.

Respect

Sacred sites remain places of living worship. Follow local caretakers, posted rules, and community guidance above any travel tip on this site.
