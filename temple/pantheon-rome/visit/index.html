Visit

Consecrated basilica; Musei Italiani timed tickets only.

Tickets

Musei Italiani — official Pantheon tickets

Only Musei Italiani / on-site official sales. Fee remodelled 2026-07-01 — verify live EUR on direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it. Worship free during liturgy. Not on Roma Pass/Omnia.

Musei Italiani — official Pantheon tickets →

Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Commonly ~09:00–19:00 (ticket office earlier; last individual entry earlier) — verify direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it. Hours shift around liturgy. Mass windows allow free worship entry — not a tourist loophole for sightseeing without respecting service.

Tickets

Official tickets only via Musei Italiani (portale.museiitaliani.it site/app) or on-site offices/machines. Info: direzionemuseiroma.cultura.gov.it / cultura.gov.it. Full price remodelled effective 1 July 2026 under MiC–Diocese agreement — verify live EUR; do not invent fares. Free categories and first Sundays per MiC rules (no online free-Sunday reservation). Not on Roma Pass/Omnia. CoopCulture "Oltre il Pantheon" is an add-on tour, not primary admission.

Dress

Shoulders and knees covered — consecrated basilica. Silence during Mass. No lying on the floor for oculus photos when it obstructs worship or safety.

Duration

45–90 minutes for a timed tourist slot. Security and slot queues add time in peak season.

Access

Centro walk from Largo di Torre Argentina / Piazza Navona; metro Barberini + walk. Timed slot at the pronaos; respect the booked window. Oculus rain is real — floor can be wet.

Tips

  • Buy only on portale.museiitaliani.it or on-site official desks/machines.
  • Verify live EUR after the 2026-07-01 fee remodel — do not invent fares.
  • Distinguish tourist ticket vs free worship during liturgy.
  • Not included in Roma Pass / Omnia.
  • No skip-the-line fantasy — timed slots only.
  • Liturgy can pause ticket sales ~1 hour before Mass.
  • Oculus rain wets the floor — watch your step.
