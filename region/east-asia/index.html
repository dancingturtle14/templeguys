Bulguksa Temple, Gyeongju

Yungang Grottoes

Mogao Caves (莫高窟), Dunhuang

UNESCO Silk Road cave crown at Dunhuang — real-name timed booking only via Academy WeChat 莫高窟参观预约网 (foreign passport / HK·Macau·Taiwan: Ticket Booking). Start at Mogao Digital Exhibition Center; no ticket office at the cliff caves. Hard-warn third-party sellers. Do not invent CNY.

East Asia

Leshan Giant Buddha (乐山大佛), Sichuan

UNESCO 71 m cliff Buddha at the Min–Dadu–Qingyi confluence — China Buddhist sculpture crown after Longmen / Yungang / Mogao. Timed booking via WeChat / Alipay 大佛旅游 (Douyin when live); walk (白天游山) ≠ boat (白天游江). Oriental Buddha Park is a separate ticketed site. Do not invent CNY; hard-warn scalpers.

Buddhist · China

East Asia

Mount Emei (峨眉山), Sichuan

UNESCO Buddhist pilgrimage mountain (Puxian / Samantabhadra) — China mountain crown and Leshan Giant Buddha serial-site peer. Scenic admission + highland sightseeing bus + optional cable cars are separate products. Book via WeChat 峨眉山景区 / mini-program 峨眉山旅游官方平台. ems517.com did not load reliably. Leidongping timed bus slots. No night hiking on posted segments. Do not invent CNY; hard-warn scalpers. Leshan ≠ Emei ticket.

Buddhist · China

East Asia

Nikkō Tōshō-gū (日光東照宮), Tochigi

UNESCO “Shrines and Temples of Nikkō” tourist crown — Tokugawa Ieyasu enshrined; Yōmeimon, Three Monkeys, Sleeping Cat. On-site shrine admission (cash common); info toshogu.jp + visitnikko.jp. Shrine ticket ≠ Tōshō-gū Museum ≠ Art Museum. Third-party vouchers often must be exchanged at Tōbu-Nikkō Station Tourist Center, not at the shrine gate. Ise remains deferred — not added. Do not invent JPY.

East Asia

Mount Kōya / Kōyasan (Kongōbu-ji + Okunoin), Wakayama

UNESCO Shingon pilgrimage mountain — Kūkai’s headquarters with Kongōbu-ji, Danjo Garan halls, and Okunoin cedar cemetery. Hall charges via Visit Wakayama or the window (koyasan.or.jp was not reachable). Okunoin approach/mausoleum area generally free (offerings/goshuin separate); Kongōbu-ji / Kondō / Konpon Daitō / Tokugawa mausoleum are separate paid halls — no single “Koyasan ticket” myth. No photography past Gobyo-bashi. Ise remains deferred — not on this page. Do not invent JPY.

Buddhist · Japan

East Asia

Shaolin Temple (少林寺), Dengfeng / Songshan, Henan

Chan Buddhism origin monastery inside Songshan Shaolin 5A scenic — global kung-fu sacred brand with living monastic halls. Official WeChat 嵩山旅游 / mini-program 漫游嵩山; consult songshancn.com. Admission ≠ cable ≠ VIP performance seats. Foreign visitors may need on-site passport verification after online hold. Do not invent CNY; hard-warn scalpers. Martial-arts show ≠ temple visit framing.

Buddhist · China

East Asia

Temple of Confucius (孔庙) / Qufu San Kong (三孔), Shandong

UNESCO Temple of Confucius + Kong Family Mansion + Cemetery (三孔) — Confucian pilgrimage capital. First confucian religion slug in SanctaWay. Real-name tickets via WeChat 文博曲阜 / 曲阜三孔景区 (and authorized OTAs when listed). Temple / Mansion / Cemetery are separate or combo products — 三孔联票 when offered. Do not invent CNY; do not fake Buddhist/Taoist consecration.

Yonghegong / Lama Temple (雍和宫), Beijing

Beijing’s most-visited living Tibetan Gelug monastery-temple — Kangxi/Yongzheng imperial conversion still active for worship. Official info yonghegong.cn; real-name tickets via WeChat 雍和宫游客信众服务 (piao.yonghegong.cn QR). No authorized third-party sellers. Hall photography banned. Do not invent CNY; passport half-price often needs on-site document window.

Lingyin Temple (灵隐寺) / Feilai Feng Scenic, Hangzhou

Hangzhou’s Chan mega beside Feilai Feng grottoes — West Lake corridor sacred landmark. Feilai Peak scenic (含灵隐寺 / 永福寺 / 韬光寺) is admission-free with mandatory real-name time-slot reservation via WeChat/Alipay mini-program 杭州灵隐飞来峰. On-site registration suspended from 2026-02-01 — use online waitlist. Do not invent CNY “ticket required” myth; hotline 0571-87969691.

East Asia

Izumo Taisha / Izumo Ōyashiro (出雲大社), Shimane

One of Japan’s oldest and most important Shinto taisha — Ōkuninushi / en-musubi (all human bonds, not romance-only). Official izumooyashiro.or.jp. Grounds / worship generally free; Treasure Hall separate paid when open. Clap form: two bows, four claps, one bow. Ise remains deferred — do not invent a casual dual-shrine day. Do not invent JPY.

Shinto · Japan

Jade Buddha Temple (玉佛禅寺), Shanghai

Shanghai living Chan / Pure Land city mega — Jade Buddha Temple. Official yufotemple.com: grounds commonly free; individual halls / incense / jade-hall products may be paid on-site — do not invent CNY as frozen forever. Feast-day crowds; not China mountain-stack filler. Metro Line 13 Jiangning Road class.

Famen Temple (法门寺), Fufeng, Shaanxi

Finger-relic pagoda complex — Tang true-body relic tradition plus modern Famen Cultural Scenic Area (合十舍利塔 / museum). Xi’an-region day-trip household without Four Sacred Mountains stack. Official WeChat 法门文化景区 real-name tickets. Do not invent CNY.
