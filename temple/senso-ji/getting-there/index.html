Sensō-ji (Asakusa Kannon)

Getting there

How visitors usually reach Senso-ji.

Take Tokyo Metro Ginza, Toei Asakusa, Tobu Skytree, or Tsukuba Express lines to Asakusa Station. From the exits it is roughly a five-minute walk to Kaminarimon, then along Nakamise to Hozomon and the Hondo.

Asakusa is also a Sumida waterbus and Tokyo Skytree day-trip hub. Those are separate paid attractions — do not assume a single combined ticket with the temple (the temple itself is free).

Expect dense pedestrian flow on Nakamise from mid-morning. If you need quieter access, aim for early hall opening or late evening when stalls thin out.

  • — Asakusa Station → ~5 min walk to Kaminarimon
  • — Metro Ginza / Toei Asakusa / Tobu / TX all serve Asakusa
  • — Skytree / waterbus are separate paid visits
  • — Dawn or late evening for thinner crowds
