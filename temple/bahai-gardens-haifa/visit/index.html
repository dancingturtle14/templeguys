Visit

Free Baháʼí Holy Place; verify segmented hours on ganbahai.org.il.

Entry

Free entry

No gate ticket. Guided tours free by reservation (booking.ganbahai.org.il). Third-party "tickets" are tours — not admission. Do not invent ILS.

Baháʼí Gardens Haifa — official visit info (free) →

Hours

Self-guided viewpoints commonly 09:00–17:00 (Upper Terraces 61 Yefe Nof; German Colony Plaza 21 Hagefen). Main Entrance 80 Hatzionut: Shrine of the Báb + inner gardens commonly 09:00–12:00; outer gardens commonly 09:00–17:00. Guided terrace tours by reservation only. Closed Baháʼí Holy Days and Yom Kippur — verify ganbahai.org.il.

Tickets

Entrance is free; official guided tours are also free. Official info: ganbahai.org.il/visit-us-haifa. Guided / group booking: booking.ganbahai.org.il. Warn: third-party "Baháʼí Gardens tickets" are tours, not admission. Do not invent ILS fares.

Dress

Clothing that covers shoulders and reaches the knees, with no tears — living Holy Place standard per ganbahai.org.il. Quiet; no food/drink beyond a water bottle; no gum or smoking.

Duration

1–2 hours for a single viewpoint entrance; allow half a morning if aiming for the shrine window at Hatzionut. Guided terrace tours are separate reserved products — check booking.ganbahai.org.il.

Access

Three public entrances at different elevations — plan transport between levels (local bus / Carmelit / taxi). Upper Terraces are viewpoint-only with stairs; Main Entrance has more paved area. Wheelchair assistance: call/email ahead per official FAQ.

Tips

  • Use ganbahai.org.il only — ignore third-party "admission tickets."
  • If you want the shrine / inner gardens, aim for the Main Entrance morning window (commonly ends at noon).
  • Check upcoming closures (Baháʼí Holy Days, Yom Kippur) before travel.
  • Book guided terrace tours ahead — reservation only; groups often need lead time.
  • Wear sensible shoes — pebbled paths can be slippery.
  • Do not treat the terraces as a full-day stair workout; access is segmented.
  • Bahjí / 'Akko (Shrine of Bahá'u'lláh) is a related but separate visit with its own hours.
