Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Pilgrimage site — daylight / evening illumination popular; exact desk hours vary — verify local notices / tourismmyanmar.org. Sunrise overnight stays common. Monsoon path slippery.

Tickets

Foreign entry fee commonly on-site / tourism channels (USD/MMK figures drift — do not invent). Truck seat separate from pagoda fee. Prefer Tourism Myanmar / on-site desks over OTA Golden Rock VIP.

Dress

Active Burmese Buddhist pilgrimage — shoulders/knees covered; shoes off on platform; gender-platform approach custom stated honestly without sensationalism; quiet near the boulder.

Duration

Full-day minimum from Yangon; overnight recommended for sunrise.

Access

Yangon to Kinpun base (bus / car ~4–5 h class) then open-truck uphill then final foot approach to the rock. Fitness and monsoon footing matter. Confirm current Myanmar travel / visa rules before you plan.

Tips

  • Confirm foreign fee on site / Tourism Myanmar — do not invent USD/MMK.
  • Truck seat is separate from the pagoda entry fee.
  • Shoulders/knees covered; shoes off on the platform.
  • State gender-platform custom honestly — follow posted local guidance.
  • Monsoon: slippery path honesty; overnight for sunrise is common.
  • Myanmar visa / travel advisories — check without fearmongering.
  • Do not invent a Bagan combo ticket.
