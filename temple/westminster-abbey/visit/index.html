Visit

This is a paid, timed visit on most days. Book the official webstore; do not invent third-party “skip” products.

Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Common pattern: Mon–Fri about 9:30am–3:30pm last entry for general visiting; Saturday often shorter; Sunday open for services, not tourist visiting. Galleries and St Margaret’s Church keep their own hours. Special services can close the Abbey — verify https://www.westminster-abbey.org/visit-us/opening-times-and-prices the week you travel.

Tickets

Buy timed general admission on the official site / https://tickets.westminster-abbey.org/. Published adult prices are often around £30+ (concessions for 65+, students, children; under-5s free) — rates change (including seasonal VAT campaigns); confirm live at booking. Tickets are typically non-refundable; the Abbey may reschedule in some disruption cases. Worship services are free to attend without a tourist ticket.

Dress

No strict tourist uniform, but dress modestly for an active church. Smart dress is requested for services. Comfortable flat shoes strongly recommended.

Duration

1.5–2 hours for main highlights; 2–3 hours with Galleries and cloisters at a relaxed pace.

Access

Security checks at entry. Large bags may be restricted — travel light. Uneven floors and steps throughout; lifts/assistance: contact visitor experience in advance if needed. Pushchairs policies can be limited in crowded nave areas — ask staff.

Tips

  • Book a morning weekday slot (Tue–Thu early) for calmer aisles.
  • Use only tickets.westminster-abbey.org or links from westminster-abbey.org.
  • Arrive a little before your timed slot; tell staff if you are late.
  • Download or collect the audio guide — tombs are dense without context.
  • If a service starts, pause sightseeing and keep silent.
  • Check whether your London Pass / city card is accepted that season before skipping the webstore.
  • Combine with nearby St Margaret’s Church hours if listed separately that day.
