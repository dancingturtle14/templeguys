Marble Jain architectural crown at Mount Abu (Vimal Vasahi / Luna Vasahi and related temples). Entry generally free (temple trust; not a typical ASI paid monument). Jama Masjid, Old Delhi

Shah Jahan’s Friday mosque — Delhi’s living Islamic tourist mega while Haram sites stay deferred. Visitor entry managed on-site; reference Delhi Tourism (delhitourism.gov.in) / Incredible India. No reliable single national e-ticket as of research — plan on-site payment + ID. Friday midday congregational prayer typically closes non-worshipper tourist entry. Modest dress; head covering for women often required. Do not invent INR; do not sell third-party skip-the-line as required entry.

Ranakpur Jain Temple (Chaumukha / Adinath), Pali, Rajasthan

15th-c. white-marble Chaumukha Adinath temple with ~1,444 carved pillars — Dilwara peer Jain tirtha in the Aravallis. Entry commonly free for visitors; camera / audio fees when charged on-site. Non-Jain tourist window commonly midday–afternoon — mornings reserved for worship. Remove shoes; no leather (belts/bags/wallets) inside. No reliable single national e-ticket — do not invent INR; do not sell third-party skip-the-line as required entry.
