Getting there

How visitors usually reach the Citadel mosque from central Cairo.

Most visitors reach the Saladin Citadel by taxi or licensed tour from central / Islamic Cairo. Allow time for plateau security queues.

Buy the MOTA Citadel ticket on egymonuments.com. Do not invent EGP from old PDFs or tour handouts.

Al-Azhar, Ibn Tulun, Sultan Hassan, the Pyramids, and Saqqara are separate MOTA or mosque products. Do not invent a single Cairo sacred pass.

Cairo’s Mamluk mosque-madrasa masterpiece opposite the Citadel. Ministry of Tourism & Antiquities egymonuments.gov.eg — ticket commonly bundles Al-Rifa’i when published. Mosque of Ahmad Ibn Tulun (مسجد أحمد بن طولون), Cairo

Oldest surviving intact mosque fabric in Cairo — Tulunid spiral-minaret mega at al-Qata'i. MOTA / egymonuments.gov.eg lists visitor hours with free admission — do not invent EGP ticket.
