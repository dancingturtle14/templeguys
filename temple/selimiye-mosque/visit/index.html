Selimiye Mosque (Selimiye Camii), Edirne

Central hill, Edirne

Quick facts

Founded
Commissioned by Selim II; completed 1575 under Mimar Sinan — his declared masterpiece
Visit length
45–90 minutes courtyard + prayer hall between prayers; museums add separate time
UNESCO
UNESCO World Heritage — Selimiye Mosque and its Social Complex
Patron / era
Islamic — Sunni imperial mosque (living congregational worship)

Visit

Free working mosque in Edirne — prayer honesty; museums separate.

Entry

Free entry

Free working mosque; museums separate; do not invent TRY.

Edirne culture / mosque notices — Selimiye (free; verify prayer closures) →

Hours

Daytime tourist entry with five-prayer + Friday noon hall closures — verify Diyanet / Edirne müftülük / edirnekulturturizm.com class notices. Courtyard freer than hall.

Tickets

Mosque entry free. Külliye museums separate paid products when open (MüzeKart / museum desk). Do not invent TRY mosque ticket. Hard-warn third-party skip-the-line.

Dress

Active imperial mosque — shoulders/knees covered; women headscarf (loan often available); remove shoes; silence during prayer.

Duration

45–90 minutes courtyard + hall between prayers; add buffer for museums.

Access

Istanbul IST / city → Edirne coach or car ~2–2.5 h (E80). Mosque on the central hill. Day-trip or overnight Edirne — not an Istanbul tram stop.

Tips

  • Mosque is free — do not invent a TRY Selimiye ticket.
  • Time around prayer and Friday noon hall closures.
  • Külliye museums are separate paid products — ask at the desk.
  • This is Edirne, not a fourth Istanbul mosque stop.
  • Modest dress; shoes off; women — scarf ready or borrow on site.
  • Hard-warn unofficial donation / prayer-bead fee approaches.
  • Allow 2–2.5 h transfer from Istanbul each way for a day-trip.
