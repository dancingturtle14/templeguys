Tourism Authority of Thailand — Wat Arun listing →

Opens the booking site we verified. Prices change — confirm on that page before you pay.

Hours

Commonly 08:00–18:00 daily; last entry often ~17:30 — verify Tourism Authority / on-site boards.

Tickets

Foreign admission commonly ฿200 cash at gate; Thais free with ID in many reports — confirm live. No need for street “closed today” touts.

Dress

Covered shoulders and knees; closed shoes with grip for steep stairs.

Duration

1–1.5 hours including climb and ferry.

Access

Very steep external stairs to terraces; not suitable for vertigo or limited mobility. Handropes help but steps are high.

Tips

  • Take the cross-river ferry from Tha Tien rather than a long tuk-tuk detour.
  • Carry small bills for the gate and ferry.
  • Climb slowly; descending is harder than ascending.
  • Photograph the prang from the east bank at sunset if stairs feel enough.
  • Ignore anyone saying the temple is closed for the day.
  • Combine with Wat Pho before or after — not both at peak noon heat.
  • MRT Isaraparp is a Thonburi-side option with a longer walk/taxi.
