Pura Luhur Uluwatu, Bali

Desa Pecatu, Kuta Selatan, Badung Regency, Bali

Quick facts

Founded
Official temple page and Indonesia Travel date early evidence to a candi kurung chronogram of 808 Saka (about 886 AD). Development is often linked to Mpu Kuturan in the 11th century. The official temple page also honours Danghyang Nirartha, who is believed to have attained moksha on these cliffs.
Visit length
The official “7 things” visitor guide says: “Allow approximately one to two hours to walk through the visitor areas… Add more time when visiting during sunset hours or attending the Kecak Dance.” That is visitor-guide wording, not a timed ticket slot.
UNESCO
Not a UNESCO World Heritage listing
Patron / era
Sad Kahyangan sea temple, dedicated to Shiva Rudra

Best time

Official sources do not publish a “best month.” Practical timing below is from official visitor articles, not a ranked season.

Morning visits can be calmer and more comfortable for walking the paths. If you also want Kecak, official text recommends arriving before 17:00 so you can enter, wear the sarong and sash, and see the cliff paths before the 18:00 show.

Piodalan falls every 210 days; access may change during ceremonies — re-verify, and do not assume a date that is not on an official notice. Tumpek Kandang / Tumpek Uye was officially noted as Saturday 5 September 2026 for one 2026 observance and returns every 210 days (that date is already past as of 2026-11-12 — pattern only). Nyepi has been announced with early closes and a 24-hour closure in a 2025 official notice; no Nyepi 2026 or 2027 dates were published on the official pages cited here — re-verify the next year on official channels.

  • Official sources do not name a best month to visit.
  • Kecak is a separate timetable: gate 16:00, show 18:00–19:00 daily — arrive before 17:00 if you also want the performance.
  • An official 2025 Nyepi notice (year-specific) closed the site for 24 hours from 06:00 on 29 March 2025 to 06:00 on 30 March 2025, and shortened Pengerupukan (28 March 2025) to 07:00–14:00, with visitors to leave by 15:00.
